# Amazon Sales Data Analysis 🛒📈

## Overview
This project is a comprehensive data analysis of an Amazon sales dataset, aimed at uncovering consumer buying patterns, evaluating fulfillment methods, and providing actionable business recommendations. By analyzing sales across categories, states, and timelines (April - July 2022), this notebook extracts critical insights to optimize inventory, marketing, and logistical strategies.

## 🔑 Key Insights

- **Top Performing Categories**: **Sets** and **Kurtas** are the undeniable backbone of the sales revenue. These categories have the highest total quantity sold and dominate high-value transactions.
- **Fulfillment Methods**: **Amazon Fulfillment** processes the vast majority of orders and outperforms Merchant fulfillment across nearly all categories. However, Amazon fulfillment also tracks higher cancellation rates. 
- **Customer Segmentation**: The bulk of orders are placed by non-B2B (regular) shoppers, contributing to the highest revenue. B2B orders remain minimal but exhibit steady transaction amounts.
- **Geographic Hotspots**: **Maharashtra** and **Karnataka** are the leading states driving revenue, with strong sales specifically in the Sets and Kurta categories.
- **Size Preferences**: Buyers opting for plus sizes (3XL-6XL) tend to spend more money per order, particularly in ethnic and western dress categories.
- **Sales Trends**: Weekends (Saturdays and Sundays) drive the highest volume of sales, while Thursdays consistently underperform.

## 📊 Multivariate & Trend Detection

- **Time Volatility**: Sales peaked in early May 2022, followed by a noticeable decline in late June. March marked the lowest performance month.
- **Correlations**: Promotions directly and positively impact revenue. However, smaller-value categories (Dupatta, Bottoms, Sarees) remain stagnant despite interventions.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Inventory Prioritization**: Double down on high-demand categories (**Sets and Kurtas**) and prioritize stock availability for standard sizes (M, L, XL). 
2. **Geographic Focus**: Target marketing campaigns and prioritize local inventory distribution in top-performing states like Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, and Telangana.
3. **Product Bundling & Upselling**: Pair niche sizes or low-performing categories (e.g., Sarees, Dupattas) with high-demand categories to boost the average order value and sell-through rate.
4. **Cancellations & Fulfillment**: Investigate the root causes of cancellations under Amazon fulfillment. Focus on last-mile delivery efficiency and customer communication to improve completion rates.
5. **Promotional Timing**: Align major promotions and discounts with high-traffic days (weekends) to maximize consumer engagement and volume.
6. **B2B Strategy**: B2B transactions are steady but minimal. Explore tailored bulk deals and partnerships to grow this segment.
